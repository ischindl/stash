================ 4. do re-captures insert FRESH identities? (copies per identity) ================
# If a re-capture changed created_at or content, the copies would NOT share an identity and
# no read-side collapse could apply - that would be verdict A. max_copies_of_one_identity is
# the count of stored rows behind ONE identity tuple.

$ docker compose -p stash exec -T postgres psql -U stash -d stash -Atc "WITH m AS (SELECT session_id, (session_id, event_type, created_at, md5(content)) AS ident, count(*) AS copies FROM history_events WHERE session_id LIKE 'fusion-task-%' GROUP BY 1,2) SELECT session_id, sum(copies) AS rows, count(*) AS identities, max(copies) AS max_copies_of_one_identity, count(*) FILTER (WHERE copies = 1) AS identities_seen_once FROM m GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 8"
