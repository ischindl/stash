=== CORRECTION to the inherited claim C4, printed rather than absorbed ===
Claim C4 said the tombstone-resurrection branch is gated on a non-null sourceAgentId and that therefore
rejection cannot occur and the lane is fail-open. The gate is real, but the conclusion is wrong:
when sourceAgentId IS populated and matches a tombstoned record created inside the sticky window, the
lane does not fail open. It writes an intake:resurrection-blocked run-audit row, soft-deletes the new
card through the store delete path - which also moves it out of `todo` into `archived` - and throws
TombstonedTaskResurrectionError out of createTask. That is the row whose verdict is `rejected`.
rejected_total_printed=1 and it is NOT 0, so this card reports it and changes no production code.
The narrower true statement for the mission-triage producer is: that producer leaves sourceAgentId empty,
so *its* cards take the drop-the-tombstone path and fail open. The lane itself is not uniformly fail-open.
