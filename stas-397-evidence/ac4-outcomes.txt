`discardedIgnoredPaths` names the two directories by name, which is the half of the bargain the old
refusal used to strike by refusing: nobody reading the audit has to guess what a released checkout
took with it. `reason` shows the sweep's own reason reached the record, and `entryCount` is the
ignored-entry count the probe saw, so a future argument about volume has a number attached.

Note on the refusal sentence: it still reads "uncommitted or ignored content present". It is now
reached only by a tree holding occupancy (`deliverable`), where the word "ignored" is a leftover of the
retired classification rather than a live reason. Renaming a user-visible error string is not this
card's call and at least three tests pin the sentence verbatim, so it stays and this line records the
inaccuracy instead of quietly fixing it.
