Risk census required by the spec: what the engine writes into a task worktree that is ignored
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
secrets-env-writer.ts materialises a git-ignored .env (332) into a task worktree, and it only
writes when the file is gitignored ("not-gitignored" is a skip reason) and records a
fingerprint file. Its source of truth is the durable SecretsStore, re-materialised at every
acquisition (worktree-acquisition.ts:49 imports writeSecretsEnvFile), so the worktree copy is a
regenerable projection, not the store. REPORTED as required: after this change a defensive
release discards that projection along with the rest of the ignored set, and it is recovered on
the next acquisition. Nothing engine-written into a worktree is a non-regenerable source of
truth in this census.
