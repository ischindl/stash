---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The released case is the invariant, stated in the file header
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A git-IGNORED path is not occupancy. Occupancy is a tracked modification, a staged change, or an
untracked path. A checkout whose only content is ignored build output releases, and that case IS
the invariant. The three refusal cases are the other half of the contract, each differing from
the released case by exactly one porcelain class, and each must keep the content it refuses to
throw away. The header says in plain words that weakening one of them trades the wedge for data
loss.
