PART 4 - THE TRAP THAT BELONGS IN THE CLOSE SUMMARY EVEN IF THE NUMERAL NEVER MOVES
================================================================================
[3] and [4] SHARE the numeral today (agent-tools.ts:801 is the same 50,000 as agent-instructions.ts:28).
Consequence, stated for the CEO before any ruling: if a ruling raises ONLY [4] to 65,536, an agent
still cannot get more than 50,000 chars of memory into its prompt through any path that writes the
field, because [3] rejects the write first. The one-line change that would enact the ruling is NOT
one line for the whole effect - it is the constant at agent-instructions.ts:28 plus the three
dashboard guards plus the imported constant's value, i.e. one NUMBER with four enforcement sites.
This card therefore keeps the numeral and prints the trap; see conflict-statement.txt.
Note the asymmetry that makes [3] the more dangerous of the two: [4] truncates (loses tail content,
reports it after this card), [3] rejects the ENTIRE write (loses the new content outright and tells
the agent, in an error string, only the number).
